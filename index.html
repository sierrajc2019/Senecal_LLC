About Our Brokerage

Nantucket isn’t just where we do business—it’s our home. Our father was a lifelong Nantucketer whose dedication to this island and its community shaped everything we do. He believed that island living was built on trust, respect for the land, and taking care of your neighbors.

Today, we are proud to carry that tradition forward. As the next generation, we manage and rent exceptional Nantucket properties with the same personal touch, local insight, and integrity our family has stood for over decades. Whether you are returning for your annual summer stay or visiting the island for the first time, we welcome you to experience Nantucket like family.
